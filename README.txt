GOOGLE INDEXING STEPS
---------------------
1. Deploy the site and open the public URL.
2. Add the site to Google Search Console.
3. Verify ownership.
4. Submit: https://YOUR_PUBLIC_URL/sitemap.xml
5. In URL Inspection, inspect your homepage and click Request Indexing.
6. Keep your public name consistent: "Sabith Salah Kp" / "Sabith Salah".
7. Link this website from your public social profiles and link those profiles back to this site.
